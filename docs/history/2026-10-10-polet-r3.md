# 2026-10-10 — «Полёт» r3: one light for weather, the land's light from below, the air before what blends (engine 2)

Branches: engine `engine2-polet-r2` (rpg, on top of r2 f9094d4), world `polet-r2` (moood-me/pavel--noch-pered-rozhdestvom-v2).
Rendering only through prod's MCP: `look_at_branch` with `engine: "engine2-polet-r2"`, `on: "gpu"`. Shaders checked
before rendering by compiling every changed material with glslangValidator (GLSL ES 3.00), assembled as three.js does
(scratchpad `glslcheck/check.mjs`).

## Causes and fixes

- **Blizzard flakes darker than their veil (engine).** The veil was coloured by the haze's light × 2, the flakes by
  the scene's light: two models, the veil a pale fog with faint dark streaks in it. Now the veil is its flakes too far off
  to draw: their colour × the light a flake gets in the air before the eye — the same `sceneLight` (what faces no way,
  `sheer` from behind, in the shadows, the light from below) worked out on the GPU each frame into a 1 × 1 target
  (`particles` pass `air`, 30 m ahead or half the veil's reach) and blitted into the lights' table's third row
  (`LIGHTS.AIR_ROW`); `haze.js veilColour()` reads it (no new sampler: the table is in every lit program; the people's
  lines and the sky's veil pass take it too). The texel holds the veil's colour itself (the flakes' colour × that light).
  The veil also lights what is in it (`veilAround` in `sceneLight`: that colour × 1 − e^(−VEIL.TOP / its reach), not
  what is inside, not the flakes nor the veil's own evaluation — no feedback from frame to frame): in a whiteout the
  snow all round is the light; the riders at 64 s were black holes in it.
- **No light from the land on what is high over it (engine).** The screen-space bounce gathers only neighbours at the
  same depth. The lighting model now has the land's light from below (`glsl/shadows.js belowLight`, in `sceneLight`):
  the land field (render/landfield.js) bakes, per level from 0.4 m cells, the land's colour — its drapes laid at their
  paintings' tails (always resident: the same however the frame was reached), premultiplied, half floats — and its
  height into a third row of its target (RGBA32UI now; no new sampler). A point gets the colour under it as wide as it
  is high (the levels whose cells are about that wide, blended), lit there by the ambient and the far lights as the
  ground faces them in the land's shadow, as much as it sees of the ground ((1 − N.y) / 2; half for what faces no way),
  through the haze and the veil between, as the look's `bounce` says (0.5 — as it is; at the first render ×0.5 was
  too weak to see). Rebaked where a level moved or the drapes
  (`land.drapes().version`: moved, posed, shown/hidden — not origin snaps) or the tails (`vt.tails()`) changed, four
  levels a frame; a held frame waits for all. Полёт's own light under the riders (`snow_fill`, keys `fill`, `fill_h`)
  removed.
- **Light band at the horizon as the snow begins (engine).** The sky's veil pass ran every ray below the horizon 30 km
  (VEIL.FAR); the land past its edge is much nearer at that angle, so a strip of sky between the land's edge and the
  horizon went veil-white while the land and the sky above were not yet (a thin snow: τ 3× more there). Below the
  horizon it now ends at the ground as far under the eye as the land under it (`u_veilBelow`). The first render still
  showed a strip of fine vertical stripes just over the horizon: the veil's curtains summed in six steps along a ray
  kilometres long at a grazing angle — each step read one curtain, a pixel apart another. Each step now takes the
  curtains it passes through averaged (their stray from the whole × √(cell / step)): far off the veil is even. What is
  left is a soft brightening toward the horizon — the far air of light snow.
- **E6, the haze glow washing out near things (engine).** The light in the air (shafts, haze glow) was added in post
  over every pixel by the opaque depth: smoke and particles write no depth, a tree's sub-pixel twigs fail the depth
  prepass — so the far sky's air (the moon's glow at full) was laid over them as if they were not there (the yard's tree
  at 15 m pale, its smoke gone). Now the main pass is: opaque → the sky → the air worked out and laid in
  (`volumetrics.lay`) → what blends, hiding as much of it as it covers and adding the air before itself
  (`airBefore(z)`: the opaque air at the pixel scaled by the haze's share before z — exact for the shafts, an estimate
  for a lamp's glow before a blended thing). Post gives the air back only over the people's lines. Also the shafts'
  phase did not keep the language's promise (toward the moon about half as bright as snow it lights): it gave 1.25×;
  now Henyey–Greenstein g 0.27 (toward 0.6, away 0.11).
- **Vertical stripes over Диканька at 640×360.** Not in the fix9 frame's pixels: its column means vary by less than
  half a level, no periodic peak (FFT) — what was seen is how the scaled sheet is shown. The MCP renders only the
  device's size (no 640×360).

## Not done

- The blizzard is one even blue at full strength in a still: its flakes are lit as the veil, so against the veil they
  do not show (as in a real whiteout); its curtains move but saturate past the first reach. How bright it is is the
  look's (`blizzard`: the moon at 0.35) — a scene matter.
- The light from below is only the land's (a scene with a relief and drapes): ground planes of a scene without a relief
  give none (the frame's bounce only); a travelling painting has no tail — none of its colour.
- `airBefore` scales the opaque air by the haze before a blended thing: exact for the shafts, an estimate for a lamp's
  glow between it and the eye.
