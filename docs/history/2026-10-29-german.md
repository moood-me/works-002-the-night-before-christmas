# The Night Before Christmas in German (2026-10-29)

Why: «Павел: ещё язык — немецкий». moood's `check` knows `de` (moood's branch `de`); this repository follows it on
its own branch `de`.

## What and how

- `worlds/the-night-before-christmas/world.json`: `"de"` added to `"languages"`; `check --write` laid out the German
  files beside the Russian ones (the world's `lang/de.json`, the five scenes' `lang/de.json`); every entry translated
  from the English, with the Russian as a second view. The world holds 12 texts: its title and description, each
  scene's title and caption. There is no game in this repository (the scenario in `docs/noch-pered-rozhdestvom/` is
  Russian design notes, not translated).
- Checked: `check` on the whole repository — "all right — 12 texts in 9 scopes".

## Names

As in German editions of Gogol («Die Nacht vor Weihnachten», from «Abende auf dem Weiler bei Dikanka»): Tschub
(«Tschubs Hütte»), Solocha («Solochas Hütte»), Dikanka; the Tsaritsa — die Zarin; Petersburg. Christmas Eve —
„der Heilige Abend“ / „die Heilige Nacht“.
